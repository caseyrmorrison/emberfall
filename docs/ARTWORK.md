# Artwork provenance

The two original anime illustrations were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this project. The pixel world, sprites, and all audio are original, deterministic code in this repository. No third-party characters or music samples are used.

## Moonlit cinematic

File: `public/art/emberfall-cinematic.png`

Prompt:

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original widescreen anime RPG cinematic key art for a browser game called Emberfall. Primary request: cinematic anime cut scene, no text. Scene: ruined moss-covered stone shrine in a magical midnight forest, fractured pale moon over distant mountains, floating golden embers and luminous teal crystal. Subject: young adult female adventurer with short silver-white hair, amber eyes, dark navy tunic and armor, long burnt-orange scarf, sword held down at side, standing in right third looking toward a beautiful mysterious glowing cyan ancient gateway on the left. Style: high quality hand-painted 1990s anime film background and detailed clean cel shaded character, expressive eyes, dramatic rim light, melancholic and hopeful, intricate vegetation, richly atmospheric, subtle film grain. Composition: 16:9 landscape, character from knees up, ample environment visible. Color palette: deep blue, muted emerald and teal, amber light. Constraints: original characters, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no UI.
