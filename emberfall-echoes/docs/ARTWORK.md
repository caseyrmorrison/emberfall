# Artwork provenance

Echoes of the Moon uses original images created for this project with the built-in ImageGen tool. The original moonlit illustration was supplied as the visual reference for each new asset. No franchise characters, game sprite packs, or sampled music are used.

## Original story artwork

- `public/art/moonlit.png`: copy of the original game's `public/art/emberfall-cinematic.png`.
- `public/art/dawn.png`: copy of the original game's `public/art/emberfall-dawn.png`.

The original generation prompts are recorded in [the parent artwork document](../../docs/ARTWORK.md). These images provide the sanctuary and illustrated story scenes.

## Moonlit clearing

Saved asset: `public/art/clearing.png`

Prompt:

Use case: illustration-story. Reference image: the supplied Emberfall anime painting establishes the exact visual world, palette, and art direction. Create a NEW landscape game environment, not a UI mockup. A lush mossy ancient forest clearing at moonlit blue hour, an ornate broken stone gateway glowing turquoise in the LEFT middle ground, weathered pillars and roots on the edges, distant mountains and broken moon visible through canopy, fireflies and fine atmospheric mist. Crucial gameplay composition: wide panoramic 16:9 establishing shot, eye level slightly elevated, the entire bottom 45 percent is a spacious uninterrupted oval moss-and-stone clearing, level ground where characters will fight, clearly visible smooth ground with scattered tiny flowers and cracks but NO large obstacles in foreground center. Trees frame only the outer edges. Center right has open negative space for a monster, center left for a player. Hand-painted detailed anime film background matching reference, rich emerald, midnight teal, luminous cyan and small warm amber accents. Beautiful soft painterly foliage and dramatic volumetric moonlight. No people, no characters, no animals, no text, no lettering, no UI. NOT pixel art, not low resolution, not voxel.

Prompt:

Fine detailed polished hand-drawn anime cel shading, smooth crisp linework, painterly highlights, not pixel art. Three-quarter side-facing RIGHT, athletic ready stance, holding a slim luminous steel sword forward and downward toward right, scarf trailing LEFT, determined expression. No large shadow, no environment, no ground plane, no lettering, no logos, no UI. Consistent full adult body proportions, beautiful detailed matching RPG heroine, readable silhouette.

## Ancient stag warden

Saved asset: `public/art/warden.png`. Full-body guardian with a transparent background.

Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: standalone full-body enemy sprite for a smooth illustrated anime action RPG on a GENUINELY TRANSPARENT background with alpha. Reference painting sets atmosphere and art direction. Subject: an ancient forest stag guardian, elegant but dangerous, massive branching antlers woven with small emerald leaves and moonflowers, dark bark-like armor over a slender deer body, pale glowing turquoise eyes, thin glowing rune veins, moss on shoulders, ornate natural silhouette. Entire animal, antlers and hooves completely in frame, side-three-quarter profile facing LEFT, poised to charge, four anatomically correct legs grounded. Refined smooth hand-painted anime-film style, richly textured painterly detail and crisp outline, moonlit highlights, luminous magical cyan accents. Centered subject occupies 85 percent of image with transparent padding; no ground, no background, no scenic environment, no text, no UI, no symbols outside the creature. This is not pixel art, not low poly, not a photograph. Beautiful original fantasy creature consistent with supplied forest artwork.

## Rendering and audio

Canvas 2D layers these images with smooth character motion, lighting, telegraphs, projectiles, and generated particles. The regular echoes are drawn procedurally. Region variants reuse the arena and guardian with color treatments. Music and sound effects are synthesized with Web Audio; no third-party audio recordings are included.
